About MarketPathAcademy

MarketPathAcademy is a free website for learning the stock market and personal finance, in English and Hindi. You do not need an account, and you can start from any topic.

Why this site exists

We believe money education should be free, simple and honest. Beginners often meet jargon, hot tips and promises of quick profit. Here, the aim is to explain how things work, show the risks as clearly as the rewards, and let you practise without risking real money.

What you will find here

  • 24 structured courses, from your first share to options, tax and trading psychology.
  • Quizzes and a final test with a certificate.
  • A paper trading simulator that uses virtual money.
  • 49 financial calculators, including SIP, EMI, FD and income tax.
  • A glossary of 380+ terms in English and Hindi.
  • Finance study notes for MBA, BBA and BBM students.
