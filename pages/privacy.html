Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

MarketPath is a static learning website. You do not need an account, and we do not ask for your name, phone number or financial details.

What is stored on your device

Some features remember your choices in your own browser (local storage). This data stays on your device; it is not sent to us. You can clear it any time from your browser settings.

  • Language and theme (light or dark).
  • Your learning progress: modules completed and quiz scores.
  • Paper-trading account, watchlist, orders and chart drawings (virtual money only).
  • Calculator inputs and the currency you choose.
  • A Finnhub API key, only if you enter one for live quotes. It is sent only to Finnhub to fetch quotes.

Third-party services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and the charting library from unpkg.com, which can see your IP address as part of normal web requests. If live quotes are switched on, requests go to Finnhub. If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve and measure ads, subject to your consent settings.

Children

The content is educational and suitable for all ages, but trading and investing accounts in India require the holder to be an adult.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if what we store changes. For questions, use the contact page.
