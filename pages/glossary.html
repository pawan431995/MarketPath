GLOSSARY

Stock market glossary

Plain-language definitions of the terms you will meet in markets, research and trading. Search by word, or filter by topic.

⌕

Acquisition

Corporate finance

One company buying control of another, with the target usually continuing as a subsidiary or being absorbed.

अधिग्रहण (Acquisition)

AIF

Mutual funds

Alternative Investment Fund: a SEBI-regulated pooled fund for sophisticated investors, covering areas such as private equity and hedge funds.

AIF

Algo trading

Trading

Using computer programs to place orders automatically according to set rules. Retail algos in India must follow SEBI and exchange rules.

एल्गो ट्रेडिंग

Alpha

Basics

The return of an investment above what its risk and benchmark would predict. It is a common measure of a fund manager's skill.

अल्फ़ा

Amalgamation

Corporate finance

Two or more companies combining, either into a new company or into one of the existing ones.

समामेलन (Amalgamation)

AMFI

Indian market

Association of Mutual Funds in India: the industry body of mutual fund houses, which also registers distributors.

AMFI

Amortisation

Banking and bonds

Paying off a loan through scheduled instalments. In accounting, spreading the cost of an intangible asset over its useful life.

परिशोधन (Amortisation)

Anchoring

Basics

A behavioural bias of relying too much on a first number seen, such as the price you paid for a share.

एंकरिंग

Angel investor

Corporate finance

A wealthy individual who invests personal money in an early-stage startup, usually in exchange for shares.

एंजेल निवेशक

Annual report

Fundamentals

A yearly document from a company with financial statements, management discussion and risks.

वार्षिक रिपोर्ट

Annuity

Banking and bonds

A series of equal payments at regular intervals, or a product that pays you a regular income in return for a lump sum.

एन्युटी

Arbitrage

Derivatives

Profiting from a price difference for the same asset in two markets by buying in one and selling in the other.

आर्बिट्राज

ASBA

Indian market

Application Supported by Blocked Amount: the IPO payment method in which the money stays in your bank account, blocked, and is debited only if you receive shares.

ASBA

ASM and GSM

Trading

Additional and Graded Surveillance Measures: exchange lists that put tighter trading limits and margins on shares showing unusual price moves.

ASM और GSM

Asset

Corporate finance

Something a person or company owns or controls that is expected to bring future benefit, such as cash, stock or machinery.

एसेट (संपत्ति)

Asset allocation

Basics

How you split money among asset classes such as equity, debt, gold and cash, based on goals and risk.

एसेट एलोकेशन

Asset bubble

Economy

Prices of an asset rising far above reasonable value on speculation, usually followed by a sharp fall.

एसेट बबल

Auditor's report

Fundamentals

An independent opinion on whether the financial statements give a true and fair view.

ऑडिटर की रिपोर्ट

AUM (assets under management)

Mutual funds

The total market value of the investments a fund or manager looks after.

AUM (प्रबंधित संपत्ति)

Authorised capital

Corporate finance

The maximum share capital a company's charter allows it to issue. Issued, subscribed and paid-up capital are parts of it.

अधिकृत पूँजी

Auto-sweep

Banking and bonds

A bank facility, also called sweep-in or flexi deposit, that moves balance above a set limit into an FD and pulls it back when the account runs short.

ऑटो-स्वीप

Average true range (ATR)

Trading

An indicator of volatility that averages each day's full trading range, including gaps.

एवरेज ट्रू रेंज (ATR)

Balance of payments

Economy

A record of all money flowing between a country and the rest of the world, made up of the current and capital accounts.

भुगतान संतुलन

Balance sheet

Fundamentals

A statement of assets, liabilities and equity at one point in time.

बैलेंस शीट

Bankruptcy and insolvency

Corporate finance

Insolvency is being unable to pay debts when due; bankruptcy is the legal process that follows. India's main law is the IBC, 2016.

दिवालियापन और इन्सॉल्वेंसी

Basis point

Banking and bonds

One hundredth of one percent (0.01%). A rate rising from 6.50% to 6.75% has risen by 25 basis points.

बेसिस पॉइंट

Bear trap

Trading

A false fall below support that draws in sellers just before the price turns back up.

बेयर ट्रैप

Benchmark

Mutual funds

A standard such as NIFTY 50 against which a fund's or portfolio's performance is compared.

बेंचमार्क

Beta

Basics

How much a share tends to move compared with the market. A beta of 1.2 suggests about 12% move for a 10% market move.

बीटा

Bid and ask

Trading

Bid is the highest price a buyer offers; ask is the lowest price a seller accepts.

बिड और आस्क

Black swan event

Economy

A rare, unexpected event with a huge impact, which seems obvious only with hindsight.

ब्लैक स्वान घटना

Black-Scholes model

Derivatives

A widely taught formula that prices options from the spot price, strike, time, interest rate and volatility.

ब्लैक-शोल्स मॉडल

Block deal

Trading

A large trade between two parties in a separate window of the exchange, within a set price range.

ब्लॉक डील

Blue-chip stocks

Basics

Shares of large, established companies known for stable business and long records of performance.

ब्लू-चिप शेयर

Bond

Banking and bonds

A loan you give to a government or company. It pays regular interest (the coupon) and returns the face value at maturity.

बॉन्ड

Bond market

Banking and bonds

The market in which governments and companies issue bonds and investors trade them.

बॉन्ड बाज़ार

Bonus issue

Basics

Free additional shares given to existing shareholders from company reserves. The total value stays the same.

बोनस इश्यू

Book building

Indian market

An IPO pricing method in which investors bid within a price band, and the final price is set from the demand received.

बुक बिल्डिंग

Book value

Fundamentals

Shareholders' equity divided by the number of shares: the accounting value per share.

बुक वैल्यू

Book value per share

Fundamentals

Shareholders' equity divided by the number of shares outstanding.

प्रति शेयर बुक वैल्यू

Bracket order

Trading

An entry order placed together with a target and a stop-loss, so that one exit cancels the other.

ब्रैकेट ऑर्डर

Break-even point

Corporate finance

The sales level at which total revenue equals total cost, so there is neither profit nor loss.

ब्रेक-ईवन बिंदु

Breakout

Technical analysis

Price moving clearly above resistance or below support, often on higher volume.

ब्रेकआउट

Broker

Basics

A registered intermediary that executes your buy and sell orders on an exchange.

ब्रोकर

Brokerage

Trading

The fee a broker charges for executing a trade.

ब्रोकरेज

BTST

Trading

Buy Today, Sell Tomorrow: selling shares before they reach your demat account, which carries a risk if the seller fails to deliver.

BTST (आज ख़रीदें, कल बेचें)

Budget

Economy

A plan of income and spending for a period. India's Union Budget sets out the government's receipts and expenditure for the year.

बजट

Budget surplus

Economy

When a government's income in a period is more than its spending.

बजट अधिशेष

Bulk deal

Trading

A trade or trades by one client in more than 0.5% of a company's listed shares in a day. Exchanges publish them.

बल्क डील

Bull and bear market

Basics

A bull market is a period of rising prices; a bear market is a sustained fall, often 20% or more from a high.

बुल और बियर मार्केट

Bull trap

Trading

A false rise above resistance that draws in buyers just before the price turns back down.

बुल ट्रैप

Buyback

Basics

When a company buys back its own shares from the market or through a tender offer, reducing the share count.

बायबैक

Call and put

Derivatives

A call gives the right to buy at the strike price; a put gives the right to sell at the strike price.

कॉल और पुट

Candlestick

Technical analysis

A chart element showing open, high, low and close for a period, coloured by direction.

कैंडलस्टिक

Capital expenditure (capex)

Fundamentals

Money spent on long-lived assets such as factories, machinery or technology.

पूँजीगत व्यय (कैपेक्स)

Capital gain

Basics

The profit made on selling an asset such as shares or property for more than its purchase cost. It is taxed by holding period.

पूँजीगत लाभ

Capital market

Banking and bonds

The market for long-term finance of more than one year, including shares and bonds. It has primary and secondary segments.

पूँजी बाज़ार

Capital structure

Corporate finance

The mix of debt and equity a company uses to finance itself.

पूँजी संरचना

Capitulation

Trading

Heavy panic selling at the end of a sharp fall, when many holders give up. It can precede a bottom, but this is known only later.

कैपिट्यूलेशन

CAPM

Basics

Capital Asset Pricing Model: expected return = risk-free rate + beta × (market return − risk-free rate).

CAPM

Cash conversion cycle

Corporate finance

The days between paying suppliers and collecting cash from customers: inventory days plus receivable days minus payable days.

कैश कन्वर्ज़न साइकिल

Cash credit

Banking and bonds

A short-term business loan limit, usually secured against stock and receivables, used to fund day-to-day working capital.

कैश क्रेडिट

Cash flow from operations

Fundamentals

Cash generated by the core business: cash received from customers minus cash paid for running costs and taxes.

संचालन कैश फ़्लो (Operating cash flow)

Cash flow statement

Fundamentals

A record of the actual cash coming in and going out, from operations, investing and financing.

कैश फ़्लो स्टेटमेंट

Certificate of deposit

Banking and bonds

A tradable short-term deposit instrument issued by banks and financial institutions, mainly to larger investors.

सर्टिफ़िकेट ऑफ़ डिपॉज़िट

Circuit limit

Indian market

A maximum daily upper or lower price move allowed for a stock. Trading may pause when it is hit.

सर्किट लिमिट

Collateral

Banking and bonds

An asset pledged to a lender as security for a loan, which the lender can claim if the loan is not repaid.

कोलैटरल (ज़मानत)

Commercial bank

Banking and bonds

A bank that takes deposits from the public and lends to people and businesses, regulated in India by the RBI.

वाणिज्यिक बैंक

Commercial paper

Banking and bonds

An unsecured short-term note issued by highly rated companies to borrow money for up to one year.

कमर्शियल पेपर

Commodity

Basics

A basic traded good such as gold, crude oil or wheat, largely the same whoever produces it.

कमोडिटी

Compound interest

Banking and bonds

Interest worked out on the principal plus interest already earned, so growth speeds up over time.

चक्रवृद्धि ब्याज

Compounding

Basics

Returns that earn their own returns over time, so growth accelerates.

कंपाउंडिंग (चक्रवृद्धि)

Conglomerate

Corporate finance

A large group that owns businesses in several unrelated industries.

कॉन्ग्लोमरेट (समूह)

Consolidated account statement (CAS)

Indian market

A monthly statement from the depositories (CDSL/NSDL) showing all shares and mutual fund units held in your name.

कंसोलिडेटेड अकाउंट स्टेटमेंट (CAS)

Contingent liabilities

Fundamentals

Possible obligations that depend on a future event, such as a lawsuit. Disclosed in the notes to accounts.

आकस्मिक देनदारियाँ

Contra fund

Mutual funds

An equity fund that takes a contrarian approach, buying what is currently out of favour.

कॉन्ट्रा फ़ंड

Contract note

Indian market

The legal record your broker sends after each trading day, listing every trade, price, brokerage, tax and charge.

कॉन्ट्रैक्ट नोट

Convertible bond

Banking and bonds

A bond the holder can exchange for a set number of the company's shares, usually paying a lower coupon in return.

कन्वर्टिबल बॉन्ड

Corporate FD

Banking and bonds

A fixed deposit with a company or NBFC rather than a bank. Rates are usually higher, and there is no DICGC deposit insurance.

कॉर्पोरेट FD

Corporate governance

Fundamentals

The rules and practices by which a company is directed and controlled, including the board and shareholder rights.

कॉर्पोरेट गवर्नेंस

Corporate tax

Economy

Tax that companies pay on their profits.

कॉर्पोरेट टैक्स

Correction

Basics

A fall of 10% or more from a recent high, within a longer uptrend.

करेक्शन

Cost of capital (WACC)

Corporate finance

The average return a company must pay its lenders and shareholders, weighted by how much of each kind of finance it uses.

पूँजी की लागत (WACC)

Cost of carry

Derivatives

The financing cost of holding an asset until a future date, minus any income from it. It links futures prices to spot prices.

कॉस्ट ऑफ़ कैरी

Cost of equity

Corporate finance

The return shareholders expect for the risk of owning a company's shares, often estimated with CAPM.

इक्विटी की लागत

Cost of goods sold (COGS)

Corporate finance

The direct cost of making or buying the goods sold in a period, such as materials and factory labour.

बेचे गए माल की लागत (COGS)

Coupon rate

Banking and bonds

The yearly interest a bond pays, as a percentage of its face value. It usually stays fixed for the bond's life.

कूपन दर

CPI (consumer price index)

Economy

An index of the prices households pay for a basket of goods and services. It is India's main measure of retail inflation.

CPI (उपभोक्ता मूल्य सूचकांक)

Credit default swap (CDS)

Derivatives

A contract in which one party pays a fee and is compensated if a specified borrower defaults, which works like insurance on debt.

क्रेडिट डिफ़ॉल्ट स्वैप (CDS)

Credit rating

Banking and bonds

An opinion from an agency such as CRISIL, ICRA or CARE on how likely a borrower is to repay. AAA is the highest grade.

क्रेडिट रेटिंग

Credit score

Basics

A number from 300 to 900, from bureaus such as CIBIL, that shows lenders how reliably you repay. Above 750 is generally good.

क्रेडिट स्कोर

Crowdfunding

Corporate finance

Raising small amounts from many people, usually online, for a project or cause.

क्राउडफ़ंडिंग

CRR (cash reserve ratio)

Economy

The share of a bank's deposits that it must keep with the RBI as cash. Raising it reduces the money banks can lend.

CRR (नकद आरक्षित अनुपात)

Cryptocurrency

Basics

A digital asset such as Bitcoin that runs on a blockchain. It is not legal tender in India, is highly volatile, and gains are heavily taxed.

क्रिप्टोकरेंसी

Cumulative FD

Banking and bonds

An FD that adds interest back to the deposit and pays everything at maturity, so the interest itself earns interest.

क्यूम्युलेटिव FD

Current account

Banking and bonds

A bank account for businesses with frequent transactions. It usually pays no interest and can come with an overdraft facility.

चालू खाता

Current account deficit

Economy

When a country pays more abroad for goods, services and transfers than it receives.

चालू खाता घाटा

Current assets

Corporate finance

Assets expected to turn into cash within a year, such as inventory, receivables and bank balances.

चालू संपत्तियाँ

Current liabilities

Corporate finance

Amounts due within a year, such as trade payables, short-term loans and taxes payable.

चालू देनदारियाँ

Current ratio

Fundamentals

Current assets divided by current liabilities. It shows whether a company can pay its short-term obligations.

चालू अनुपात (Current ratio)

Dabba trading

Indian market

Illegal betting on share prices outside a recognised exchange. Investors have no protection or recourse.

डब्बा ट्रेडिंग

Dark pool

Trading

A private venue abroad for large trades, where orders are not visible to the public before execution.

डार्क पूल

DDPI

Indian market

Demat Debit and Pledge Instruction: a limited authorisation that lets a broker debit shares from your demat account to settle sales you make.

DDPI

Debenture

Banking and bonds

A company's debt instrument, which may be secured or unsecured, and which pays a fixed rate of interest.

डिबेंचर

Debt fund

Mutual funds

A mutual fund that invests in bonds and money market instruments. It is generally steadier than equity but not risk-free.

डेट फ़ंड

Debt-to-equity

Fundamentals

Total debt divided by shareholders' equity. Higher values mean more borrowing.

डेट-टू-इक्विटी

Deflation

Economy

A general fall in prices across the economy. It can make people delay spending and increases the real burden of debt.

अपस्फीति (Deflation)

Demat account

Indian market

An account that holds your shares in electronic form.

डीमैट खाता

Demerger (spin-off)

Corporate finance

Separating a division into its own company, with existing shareholders usually receiving shares in it.

डीमर्जर (स्पिन-ऑफ़)

Demonetisation

Economy

Withdrawing the legal-tender status of a currency note, as India did with its old ₹500 and ₹1,000 notes in November 2016.

विमुद्रीकरण (Demonetisation)

Deposit insurance (DICGC)

Banking and bonds

Cover from an RBI subsidiary that protects bank deposits up to a limit, currently ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank, including interest.

जमा बीमा (DICGC)

Depreciation

Corporate finance

Spreading the cost of a fixed asset over its useful life as an expense. It reduces profit but involves no cash outflow.

मूल्यह्रास (Depreciation)

Derivative

Derivatives

A contract whose value comes from an underlying asset such as a share, index, commodity or currency.

डेरिवेटिव

Dilution

Corporate finance

The fall in an existing shareholder's percentage ownership when a company issues new shares.

डाइल्यूशन

Direct plan

Mutual funds

A mutual fund plan bought straight from the fund house with no distributor commission, so its expense ratio is lower.

डायरेक्ट प्लान

Direct tax

Economy

Tax paid directly to the government by the person it is levied on, such as income tax and corporate tax.

प्रत्यक्ष कर

Discounted cash flow (DCF)

Fundamentals

A valuation method that estimates future cash flows and discounts them to today's value.

डिस्काउंटेड कैश फ़्लो (DCF)

Disinflation

Economy

A slowing in the rate of inflation. Prices are still rising, but more slowly than before.

डिसइन्फ़्लेशन

Divergence

Technical analysis

When price and an indicator move in opposite directions, such as a new price high with a lower indicator high. It can warn that momentum is fading.

डाइवर्जेंस

Diversification

Basics

Spreading money across different investments to reduce the damage from any one of them.

विविधीकरण

Divestment

Corporate finance

Selling a stake or business unit. In India the term is also used for the government selling its stake in public-sector companies.

विनिवेश (Divestment)

Dividend

Basics

A share of profit a company pays to its shareholders, usually per share.

डिविडेंड (लाभांश)

Dividend yield

Fundamentals

Annual dividend per share divided by share price, shown as a percentage.

डिविडेंड यील्ड

Drawdown

Trading

The fall from a peak to a later low in your account or in a price, shown as a percentage.

ड्रॉडाउन

DRHP

Indian market

Draft Red Herring Prospectus: the document a company files with SEBI before an IPO, describing its business, finances and risks.

DRHP

Due diligence

Corporate finance

A detailed check of a company's finances, legal position and risks before an investment or acquisition.

ड्यू डिलिजेंस

DuPont analysis

Corporate finance

Breaking ROE into net margin × asset turnover × equity multiplier to see what drives a company's return.

ड्यूपॉन्ट विश्लेषण

EBIT

Corporate finance

Earnings before interest and tax: profit from operations before finance costs and tax.

EBIT

EBITDA

Fundamentals

Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation. A measure of operating performance.

EBITDA

Economic moat

Corporate finance

A lasting advantage, such as brand, scale or switching costs, that protects a company's profits from competitors.

इकोनॉमिक मोट

Economic order quantity (EOQ)

Corporate finance

The order size that minimises the total cost of ordering and holding inventory.

इकोनॉमिक ऑर्डर क्वांटिटी (EOQ)

Economies of scale

Corporate finance

Cost per unit falling as output grows, because fixed costs are spread over more units.

बड़े पैमाने की मितव्ययिता

Efficient frontier

Basics

In portfolio theory, the set of portfolios offering the highest expected return for each level of risk.

एफ़िशिएंट फ़्रंटियर

ELSS

Mutual funds

Equity Linked Savings Scheme: an equity mutual fund with a lock-in period and tax benefits under Indian tax law. Check current rules.

ELSS

Emergency fund

Basics

Cash or very safe, liquid savings to cover 3 to 6 months of expenses. Keep it separate from investments.

इमरजेंसी फ़ंड

EMI

Basics

Equated monthly instalment: the fixed monthly amount that repays a loan, made up of interest and principal.

EMI (मासिक किस्त)

Endowment policy

Banking and bonds

Life insurance that pays on death or at maturity. Returns are often low, and the cover is small for the premium paid.

एंडोमेंट पॉलिसी

EPF

Indian market

Employees' Provident Fund: retirement savings for salaried employees, with contributions from both employee and employer.

EPF (कर्मचारी भविष्य निधि)

EPS (earnings per share)

Fundamentals

Net profit divided by the number of shares outstanding.

EPS (प्रति शेयर आय)

Equity

Basics

Ownership in a company through shares. Also used for the value left for shareholders after debts.

इक्विटी

Equity fund

Mutual funds

A mutual fund that invests mainly in shares. It suits long horizons and carries market risk.

इक्विटी फ़ंड

ESG investing

Basics

Weighing environmental, social and governance factors alongside financial ones when choosing investments.

ESG निवेश

ESOP

Corporate finance

Employee Stock Option Plan: a company benefit that gives employees the right to buy its shares at a set price after a vesting period.

ESOP

ETF

Mutual funds

Exchange-Traded Fund: a fund, usually tracking an index or gold, whose units trade on an exchange like shares.

ETF (एक्सचेंज ट्रेडेड फ़ंड)

EV/EBITDA

Fundamentals

Enterprise value (market cap plus debt minus cash) divided by EBITDA. A valuation measure that is neutral to debt levels.

EV/EBITDA

Ex-date

Basics

The first day a share trades without the right to an upcoming dividend or corporate action. The price usually adjusts on this day.

एक्स-डेट

Exceptional items

Fundamentals

Large, unusual or one-time gains or losses shown separately in results, such as a property sale or a write-down.

असाधारण मदें (Exceptional items)

Exchange rate

Economy

The price of one currency in terms of another, such as rupees per US dollar.

विनिमय दर

Exit load

Mutual funds

A fee some mutual funds charge when you redeem units within a set period.

एग्ज़िट लोड

Expense ratio

Mutual funds

The yearly fee a fund charges, as a percentage of assets.

एक्सपेंस रेशियो

Expiry

Derivatives

The date on which a futures or option contract ends.

एक्सपायरी

Face value

Corporate finance

The stated value of a share or bond in the company's records, such as ₹10 per share. It often differs widely from the market price.

अंकित मूल्य (Face value)

FCNR deposit

Banking and bonds

A fixed deposit for non-residents held in a foreign currency, which protects the depositor from rupee exchange-rate movements.

FCNR डिपॉज़िट

FII and DII

Basics

Foreign Institutional Investors and Domestic Institutional Investors. Their buying and selling is watched as a market signal.

FII और DII

Financial leverage

Corporate finance

Using fixed-cost debt to raise returns to shareholders. It magnifies both profits and losses per share.

वित्तीय लीवरेज

Fiscal deficit

Economy

How much a government's total spending exceeds its income excluding borrowing. It shows how much it must borrow.

राजकोषीय घाटा

Fiscal policy

Economy

The government's use of spending and taxation to influence the economy.

राजकोषीय नीति

Fixed asset

Corporate finance

A long-term asset used in the business rather than for sale, such as land, buildings or machinery.

स्थायी संपत्ति (Fixed asset)

Fixed deposit (FD)

Banking and bonds

The rate the RBI pays banks when it borrows from them against securities, which absorbs excess money from the system.

रिवर्स रेपो रेट

Rights issue

Basics

An offer to existing shareholders to buy new shares, usually at a discount, in proportion to their holding.

राइट्स इश्यू

Risk appetite

Basics

How much loss and fluctuation you are willing and able to accept in pursuit of returns.

जोखिम क्षमता (Risk appetite)

Risk-reward ratio

Technical analysis

The potential profit of a trade compared with its potential loss. A 1:2 ratio means ₹2 of reward for each ₹1 risked.

जोखिम-इनाम अनुपात

ROCE (return on capital employed)

Corporate finance

EBIT divided by capital employed (equity plus long-term debt). It shows how well a company uses all its long-term funds.

ROCE (नियोजित पूँजी पर रिटर्न)

ROE (return on equity)

Fundamentals

Net profit divided by shareholders' equity. It shows how well the company uses shareholder money.

ROE (इक्विटी पर रिटर्न)

RSI

Technical analysis

Relative Strength Index: a 0 to 100 momentum measure. Above 70 is often overbought; below 30 oversold.

RSI

Rupee cost averaging

Mutual funds

Buying a fixed amount regularly, so you buy more units when prices are low and fewer when high. It does not guarantee a profit.

रुपी कॉस्ट एवरेजिंग

Savings account

Banking and bonds

A bank account for personal money that pays modest interest and allows easy withdrawals.

बचत खाता

SCORES

Indian market

SEBI's online complaints system (scores.sebi.gov.in) for complaints against listed companies, brokers and other market entities.

SCORES

SEBI

Indian market

The Securities and Exchange Board of India, the regulator that protects investors and oversees markets.

SEBI

SEBI-registered adviser (RIA / RA)

Indian market

An Investment Adviser or Research Analyst registered with SEBI. Only they may charge for personalised advice or stock recommendations.

SEBI-रजिस्टर्ड सलाहकार (RIA / RA)

Secondary market

Basics

Where investors trade existing securities with each other on an exchange. The company gets no money from these trades.

द्वितीयक बाज़ार

Sector fund

Mutual funds

An equity fund that invests in one sector, such as banking or technology. It is less diversified and so riskier.

सेक्टर फ़ंड

Secured loan

Banking and bonds

A loan backed by an asset such as a house, car or gold, which the lender can sell if you default. Rates are usually lower.

सिक्योर्ड लोन

Securities

Basics

Tradable financial instruments such as shares, bonds, debentures, mutual fund units and derivatives.

प्रतिभूतियाँ (Securities)

SENSEX

Indian market

An index of 30 large companies listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE).

SENSEX

Sentiment indicators

Trading

Measures of the market's mood, such as India VIX or the put-call ratio.

सेंटीमेंट इंडिकेटर

Settlement (T+1)

Indian market

The process of transferring shares and money after a trade. In India, equity trades settle one trading day after the trade (T+1).

सेटलमेंट (T+1)

Share (stock)

Basics

A unit of ownership in a company. Holding shares makes you a part-owner.

शेयर (स्टॉक)

Shareholder

Corporate finance

A person or institution that owns shares in a company and so holds part of its ownership.

शेयरधारक

Sharpe ratio

Basics

Return above the risk-free rate, divided by standard deviation. It shows the return earned per unit of total risk.

शार्प अनुपात

Short selling

Trading

Selling shares you do not own, to buy them back later at a lower price. It carries unlimited loss potential if price rises.

शॉर्ट सेलिंग

Shrinkflation

Economy

A product getting smaller at the same price, which is a hidden form of price rise.

श्रिंकफ़्लेशन

Simple interest

Banking and bonds

Interest worked out only on the original principal: principal × rate × time.

साधारण ब्याज

SIP

Mutual funds

Systematic Investment Plan: investing a fixed amount at regular intervals into a fund.

SIP

Slippage

Trading

The difference between the expected price of a trade and the price it actually fills at.

स्लिपेज

SLR (statutory liquidity ratio)

Economy

The share of a bank's deposits that it must hold in liquid assets such as cash, gold or government securities.

SLR (वैधानिक तरलता अनुपात)

SMART ODR

Indian market

SEBI's online dispute resolution platform (smartodr.in) for conciliation and arbitration with market entities when a complaint is not resolved.

SMART ODR

Solvency ratio

Corporate finance

A measure of whether a business can meet its long-term obligations, such as debt-to-equity or interest coverage.

सॉल्वेंसी अनुपात

Sortino ratio

Basics

Like the Sharpe ratio, but it counts only downside volatility as risk.

सॉर्टिनो अनुपात

Spread

Trading

The gap between bid and ask. Narrow spreads usually mean a liquid stock.

स्प्रेड

Stagflation

Economy

High inflation together with weak growth and high unemployment, which is hard for policymakers to treat.

स्टैगफ़्लेशन

Standard deviation

Basics

A statistical measure of how widely returns vary around their average. It is the most common measure of investment risk.

मानक विचलन

Stock market

Basics

The regulated marketplace where shares of listed companies are issued and traded through stock exchanges.

शेयर बाज़ार

Stock split

Basics

Dividing existing shares into more shares. The price per share falls proportionally; the total value stays the same.

स्टॉक स्प्लिट

Stop-loss order

Trading

An order that becomes a market or limit order when price reaches a set level, to limit a loss. It is not guaranteed to fill at that level.

स्टॉप-लॉस ऑर्डर

STP and SWP

Mutual funds

A systematic transfer plan moves money regularly from one fund to another; a systematic withdrawal plan pays you a regular amount from a fund.

STP और SWP

Strike price

Derivatives

The price at which an option can be exercised.

स्ट्राइक प्राइस

STT (Securities Transaction Tax)

Indian market

A tax charged by the government on securities transactions, varying by trade type.

STT (सिक्योरिटीज़ ट्रांज़ैक्शन टैक्स)

Subsidiary

Corporate finance

A company controlled by another (its parent), usually through more than half of its voting shares.

सहायक कंपनी

Subsidy

Economy

Government support that lowers the cost of something, such as fertiliser or fuel, for producers or consumers.

सब्सिडी

Support

Technical analysis

A price level where buying has tended to appear and stop further falls.

सपोर्ट

Surcharge

Economy

An extra charge added on top of income tax for people or companies with income above set levels.

अधिभार (Surcharge)

Swap

Derivatives

A contract to exchange cash flows, such as fixed-rate interest for floating-rate interest, for a set period.

स्वैप

Swing trading

Trading

Holding trades for days to weeks to capture a price swing.

स्विंग ट्रेडिंग

Tax-loss harvesting

Indian market

Selling investments at a loss to set the loss against taxable gains, within the tax rules.

टैक्स-लॉस हार्वेस्टिंग

Tax-saving FD

Banking and bonds

A five-year lock-in bank deposit whose investment can be claimed as a deduction under the old tax regime. The interest is still taxable.

टैक्स-सेविंग FD

TDS

Banking and bonds

Tax Deducted at Source: tax taken out by the payer, such as a bank on FD interest, before paying you. You can claim it when filing your return.

TDS (स्रोत पर कर कटौती)

Term insurance

Basics

Pure life cover that pays a large sum to your family if you die during the policy term, with no return if you survive.

टर्म इंश्योरेंस

Trading account

Indian market

The account used to place buy and sell orders through a broker.

ट्रेडिंग खाता

Treasury bill (T-bill)

Banking and bonds

A short-term central government security of up to one year, issued at a discount and redeemed at face value.

ट्रेज़री बिल (T-bill)

Trend

Technical analysis

The general direction of price: up (higher highs and lows), down (lower highs and lows), or sideways.

ट्रेंड

Trial balance

Corporate finance

A list of all ledger balances at a date, used to check that total debits equal total credits before preparing accounts.

ट्रायल बैलेंस

Underlying asset

Derivatives

The share, index, commodity or currency on which a derivative contract is based.

अंतर्निहित एसेट

Unsecured loan

Banking and bonds

A loan with no asset pledged, such as a personal loan or credit card balance. Rates are usually higher.

अनसिक्योर्ड लोन

UPI

Banking and bonds

Unified Payments Interface: India's instant mobile payment system, which moves money between bank accounts through an app.

UPI

Value investing

Basics

Buying shares that seem priced below their intrinsic value and waiting for the market to recognise it.

वैल्यू निवेश

Venture capital

Corporate finance

Professional investment in young, fast-growing companies in return for equity, at high risk.

वेंचर कैपिटल

Volatility

Basics

How much and how quickly a price moves. Higher volatility means bigger swings in both directions.

अस्थिरता (वोलैटिलिटी)

Volume

Basics

The number of shares traded in a period. High volume means more participation.

वॉल्यूम

Working capital

Fundamentals

Current assets minus current liabilities. The money available to run daily operations.

वर्किंग कैपिटल

WPI (wholesale price index)

Economy

An index of prices at the wholesale level, which tracks price changes before goods reach consumers.

WPI (थोक मूल्य सूचकांक)

WTO

Economy

World Trade Organization: the global body that sets rules for trade between countries and settles trade disputes.

WTO (विश्व व्यापार संगठन)

XIRR

Mutual funds

A yearly return worked out for cash flows on irregular dates. It is the right way to measure SIP returns.

XIRR

Yield curve

Banking and bonds

A chart of bond yields against time to maturity. An inverted curve, where short rates exceed long rates, is watched as a warning of slowdown.

यील्ड कर्व

Yield to maturity (YTM)

Banking and bonds

The total yearly return you earn if you buy a bond at today's price and hold it to maturity. Price and yield move in opposite directions.

यील्ड टू मैच्योरिटी (YTM)
