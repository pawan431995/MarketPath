GLOSSARY

Stock market glossary

Plain-language definitions of the terms you will meet in markets, research and trading. Search by word, or filter by topic.

⌕

Annual report

Fundamentals

A yearly document from a company with financial statements, management discussion and risks.

वार्षिक रिपोर्ट

ASBA

Indian market

Application Supported by Blocked Amount: the IPO payment method in which the money stays in your bank account, blocked, and is debited only if you receive shares.

ASBA

Asset allocation

Basics

How you split money among asset classes such as equity, debt, gold and cash, based on goals and risk.

एसेट एलोकेशन

Auditor's report

Fundamentals

An independent opinion on whether the financial statements give a true and fair view.

ऑडिटर की रिपोर्ट

Balance sheet

Fundamentals

A statement of assets, liabilities and equity at one point in time.

बैलेंस शीट

Bid and ask

Trading

Bid is the highest price a buyer offers; ask is the lowest price a seller accepts.

बिड और आस्क

Bonus issue

Basics

Free additional shares given to existing shareholders from company reserves. The total value stays the same.

बोनस इश्यू

Book value

Fundamentals

Shareholders' equity divided by the number of shares: the accounting value per share.

बुक वैल्यू

Book value per share

Fundamentals

Shareholders' equity divided by the number of shares outstanding.

प्रति शेयर बुक वैल्यू

Breakout

Technical analysis

Price moving clearly above resistance or below support, often on higher volume.

ब्रेकआउट

Broker

Basics

A registered intermediary that executes your buy and sell orders on an exchange.

ब्रोकर

Brokerage

Trading

The fee a broker charges for executing a trade.

ब्रोकरेज

Bull and bear market

Basics

A bull market is a period of rising prices; a bear market is a sustained fall, often 20% or more from a high.

बुल और बियर मार्केट

Buyback

Basics

When a company buys back its own shares from the market or through a tender offer, reducing the share count.

बायबैक

Call and put

Derivatives

A call gives the right to buy at the strike price; a put gives the right to sell at the strike price.

कॉल और पुट

Candlestick

Technical analysis

A chart element showing open, high, low and close for a period, coloured by direction.

कैंडलस्टिक

Capital expenditure (capex)

Fundamentals

Money spent on long-lived assets such as factories, machinery or technology.

पूँजीगत व्यय (कैपेक्स)

Cash flow from operations

Fundamentals

Cash generated by the core business: cash received from customers minus cash paid for running costs and taxes.

संचालन कैश फ़्लो (Operating cash flow)

Cash flow statement

Fundamentals

A record of the actual cash coming in and going out, from operations, investing and financing.

कैश फ़्लो स्टेटमेंट

Circuit limit

Indian market

A maximum daily upper or lower price move allowed for a stock. Trading may pause when it is hit.

सर्किट लिमिट

Compounding

Basics

Returns that earn their own returns over time, so growth accelerates.

कंपाउंडिंग (चक्रवृद्धि)

Consolidated account statement (CAS)

Indian market

A monthly statement from the depositories (CDSL/NSDL) showing all shares and mutual fund units held in your name.

कंसोलिडेटेड अकाउंट स्टेटमेंट (CAS)

Contingent liabilities

Fundamentals

Possible obligations that depend on a future event, such as a lawsuit. Disclosed in the notes to accounts.

आकस्मिक देनदारियाँ

Contract note

Indian market

The legal record your broker sends after each trading day, listing every trade, price, brokerage, tax and charge.

कॉन्ट्रैक्ट नोट

Corporate governance

Fundamentals

The rules and practices by which a company is directed and controlled, including the board and shareholder rights.

कॉर्पोरेट गवर्नेंस

Correction

Basics

A fall of 10% or more from a recent high, within a longer uptrend.

करेक्शन

Current ratio

Fundamentals

Current assets divided by current liabilities. It shows whether a company can pay its short-term obligations.

चालू अनुपात (Current ratio)

Dabba trading

Indian market

Illegal betting on share prices outside a recognised exchange. Investors have no protection or recourse.

डब्बा ट्रेडिंग

Debt-to-equity

Fundamentals

Total debt divided by shareholders' equity. Higher values mean more borrowing.

डेट-टू-इक्विटी

Demat account

Indian market

An account that holds your shares in electronic form.

डीमैट खाता

Discounted cash flow (DCF)

Fundamentals

A valuation method that estimates future cash flows and discounts them to today's value.

डिस्काउंटेड कैश फ़्लो (DCF)

Divergence

Technical analysis

When price and an indicator move in opposite directions, such as a new price high with a lower indicator high. It can warn that momentum is fading.

डाइवर्जेंस

Diversification

Basics

Spreading money across different investments to reduce the damage from any one of them.

विविधीकरण

Dividend

Basics

A share of profit a company pays to its shareholders, usually per share.

डिविडेंड (लाभांश)

Dividend yield

Fundamentals

Annual dividend per share divided by share price, shown as a percentage.

डिविडेंड यील्ड

Drawdown

Trading

The fall from a peak to a later low in your account or in a price, shown as a percentage.

ड्रॉडाउन

EBITDA

Fundamentals

Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation. A measure of operating performance.

EBITDA

ELSS

Mutual funds

Equity Linked Savings Scheme: an equity mutual fund with a lock-in period and tax benefits under Indian tax law. Check current rules.

ELSS

Emergency fund

Basics

Cash or very safe, liquid savings to cover 3 to 6 months of expenses. Keep it separate from investments.

इमरजेंसी फ़ंड

EPS (earnings per share)

Fundamentals

Net profit divided by the number of shares outstanding.

EPS (प्रति शेयर आय)

Equity

Basics

Ownership in a company through shares. Also used for the value left for shareholders after debts.

इक्विटी

EV/EBITDA

Fundamentals

Enterprise value (market cap plus debt minus cash) divided by EBITDA. A valuation measure that is neutral to debt levels.

EV/EBITDA

Ex-date

Basics

The first day a share trades without the right to an upcoming dividend or corporate action. The price usually adjusts on this day.

एक्स-डेट

Exceptional items

Fundamentals

Large, unusual or one-time gains or losses shown separately in results, such as a property sale or a write-down.

असाधारण मदें (Exceptional items)

Expense ratio

Mutual funds

The yearly fee a fund charges, as a percentage of assets.

एक्सपेंस रेशियो

Expiry

Derivatives

The date on which a futures or option contract ends.

एक्सपायरी

FII and DII

Basics

Foreign Institutional Investors and Domestic Institutional Investors. Their buying and selling is watched as a market signal.

FII और DII

Free cash flow

Fundamentals

Operating cash flow minus capital expenditure. The cash left after keeping the business running and growing.

फ़्री कैश फ़्लो (FCF)

Free float

Mutual funds

The part of a company's shares not held by promoters or locked in, and so available for normal trading.

फ़्री फ़्लोट

Futures

Derivatives

A contract to buy or sell an asset at a set price on a future date. Both sides have obligations.

फ्यूचर्स

Goodwill

Fundamentals

The extra amount paid to buy a business above the value of its net assets. Recorded on the balance sheet.

गुडविल

Greeks (delta, theta)

Derivatives

Measures of option sensitivity. Delta measures price sensitivity; theta measures time decay.

ग्रीक्स (डेल्टा, थीटा)

Grey market premium (GMP)

Indian market

An unofficial, unregulated price quoted for IPO shares before listing. It is not reliable and has no legal standing.

ग्रे मार्केट प्रीमियम (GMP)

Implied volatility (IV)

Derivatives

The volatility the option price implies for the future. Higher IV makes options more expensive.

इम्प्लाइड वोलैटिलिटी (IV)

Index

Basics

A measure that tracks a selected group of shares, such as NIFTY 50 or SENSEX.

इंडेक्स

Index fund

Mutual funds

A fund that copies an index such as NIFTY 50, usually with low costs.

इंडेक्स फंड

Inflation

Basics

The general rise in prices over time, which reduces what your money can buy.

महँगाई (इन्फ़्लेशन)

Intraday

Trading

Trades opened and closed within the same trading session. Positions must be squared off before the market closes.

इंट्राडे

IPO

Basics

Initial Public Offering: a company's first sale of shares to the public, after which it is listed.

IPO (प्रारंभिक सार्वजनिक निर्गम)

ITM, ATM, OTM

Derivatives

In the money (has intrinsic value), at the money (strike near price), out of the money (no intrinsic value).

ITM, ATM, OTM

KYC

Indian market

Know Your Customer: identity and address verification required before you can invest.

KYC

Leverage

Trading

Using borrowed money or margin to take a bigger position. It magnifies both gains and losses.

लीवरेज

Limit order

Trading

An order to buy at or below, or sell at or above, a set price. It may not execute.

लिमिट ऑर्डर

Liquidity

Trading

How easily an asset can be bought or sold without moving its price much.

लिक्विडिटी

Lot size

Derivatives

The fixed quantity in one derivatives contract. You trade in whole lots.

लॉट साइज़

MACD

Technical analysis

Moving Average Convergence Divergence: a momentum indicator built from two moving averages and a signal line.

MACD

Margin

Trading

The money you must deposit with a broker to hold a leveraged position.

मार्जिन

Margin of safety

Fundamentals

The gap between estimated value and price. Buying with a margin protects you if your estimate is too optimistic.

सुरक्षा मार्जिन (Margin of safety)

Market capitalisation (market cap)

Basics

Share price multiplied by the number of shares outstanding. Companies are grouped as large, mid or small cap.

मार्केट कैप

Market order

Trading

An order to buy or sell immediately at the best available price. The final price may differ from the displayed one.

मार्केट ऑर्डर

Market-wide circuit breaker

Indian market

A halt in trading across the whole market when the NIFTY 50 or SENSEX moves 10%, 15% or 20% in a day.

मार्केट-वाइड सर्किट ब्रेकर

Moving average

Technical analysis

The average price over a set number of periods, used to smooth price and show trend.

मूविंग एवरेज

Muhurat trading

Indian market

A short special trading session held on Diwali, considered auspicious for starting the new Hindu financial year.

मुहूर्त ट्रेडिंग

Mutual fund

Mutual funds

A pooled investment managed by a professional that holds a mix of securities.

म्यूचुअल फंड

NAV (net asset value)

Mutual funds

The value of one unit of a mutual fund, calculated once a day after the market closes.

NAV (शुद्ध परिसंपत्ति मूल्य)

Net profit

Fundamentals

What is left from revenue after all costs, interest and taxes are paid.

शुद्ध मुनाफ़ा

NIFTY 50

Indian market

An index of 50 large companies listed on the National Stock Exchange (NSE).

NIFTY 50

Nomination

Indian market

Naming the person who should receive your shares, funds or account balance after your death. You can name more than one person and split the share.

नॉमिनेशन

NSE and BSE

Indian market

India's two main stock exchanges, where listed shares are bought and sold.

NSE और BSE

Open interest

Derivatives

The number of outstanding derivative contracts that have not been closed or settled.

ओपन इंटरेस्ट

Operating margin

Fundamentals

Operating profit divided by revenue. How much of each rupee of sales is left after running costs.

ऑपरेटिंग मार्जिन

Option

Derivatives

A contract giving the buyer the right, but not the obligation, to buy (call) or sell (put) at a set price before expiry.

ऑप्शन

Order

Trading

An instruction to buy or sell a security at a given price or at the best available price.

ऑर्डर

P/E ratio

Fundamentals

Share price divided by earnings per share. It shows how much investors pay for each rupee of earnings.

P/E अनुपात

PEG ratio

Fundamentals

P/E ratio divided by the expected earnings growth rate. A rough guide to whether a growth stock is fairly priced.

PEG अनुपात

Position sizing

Trading

Deciding how many shares to buy based on the maximum loss you accept and the distance to your stop.

पोज़िशन साइज़िंग

Pre-open session

Indian market

The 9:00–9:15 am session in which orders are collected and matched to set a fair opening price before normal trading starts.

प्री-ओपन सेशन

Premium

Derivatives

The price paid by the option buyer to the seller.

प्रीमियम

Promoter

Basics

The people or group who founded or control a company. Promoter holding is the stake they own.

प्रमोटर

Promoter pledge

Fundamentals

Promoters using their shares as security for a loan. If the price falls, lenders may sell the pledged shares.

प्रमोटर गिरवी (Pledge)

Pump and dump

Indian market

A fraud in which operators buy a small stock, hype it to push the price up, and sell to the buyers they attract.

पंप एंड डंप

Record date

Basics

The date on which shareholders must be on the register to receive a dividend or benefit.

रिकॉर्ड डेट

Related-party transactions

Fundamentals

Dealings between a company and its promoters, directors or companies they control. They must be disclosed.

संबंधित-पक्ष लेनदेन

Reserves

Fundamentals

Profits kept in the business over time instead of being paid out as dividends.

रिज़र्व

Resistance

Technical analysis

A price level where selling has tended to appear and stop further rises.

रेज़िस्टेंस

Revenue (sales)

Fundamentals

Total income from selling goods or services before any costs are deducted.

राजस्व (बिक्री)

Rights issue

Basics

An offer to existing shareholders to buy new shares, usually at a discount, in proportion to their holding.

राइट्स इश्यू

Risk-reward ratio

Technical analysis

The potential profit of a trade compared with its potential loss. A 1:2 ratio means ₹2 of reward for each ₹1 risked.

जोखिम-इनाम अनुपात

ROE (return on equity)

Fundamentals

Net profit divided by shareholders' equity. It shows how well the company uses shareholder money.

ROE (इक्विटी पर रिटर्न)

RSI

Technical analysis

Relative Strength Index: a 0 to 100 momentum measure. Above 70 is often overbought; below 30 oversold.

RSI

Rupee cost averaging

Mutual funds

Buying a fixed amount regularly, so you buy more units when prices are low and fewer when high. It does not guarantee a profit.

रुपी कॉस्ट एवरेजिंग

SCORES

Indian market

SEBI's online complaints system (scores.sebi.gov.in) for complaints against listed companies, brokers and other market entities.

SCORES

SEBI

Indian market

The Securities and Exchange Board of India, the regulator that protects investors and oversees markets.

SEBI

SEBI-registered adviser (RIA / RA)

Indian market

An Investment Adviser or Research Analyst registered with SEBI. Only they may charge for personalised advice or stock recommendations.

SEBI-रजिस्टर्ड सलाहकार (RIA / RA)

SENSEX

Indian market

An index of 30 large companies listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE).

SENSEX

Settlement (T+1)

Indian market

The process of transferring shares and money after a trade. In India, equity trades settle one trading day after the trade (T+1).

सेटलमेंट (T+1)

Share (stock)

Basics

A unit of ownership in a company. Holding shares makes you a part-owner.

शेयर (स्टॉक)

Short selling

Trading

Selling shares you do not own, to buy them back later at a lower price. It carries unlimited loss potential if price rises.

शॉर्ट सेलिंग

SIP

Mutual funds

Systematic Investment Plan: investing a fixed amount at regular intervals into a fund.

SIP

Slippage

Trading

The difference between the expected price of a trade and the price it actually fills at.

स्लिपेज

SMART ODR

Indian market

SEBI's online dispute resolution platform (smartodr.in) for conciliation and arbitration with market entities when a complaint is not resolved.

SMART ODR

Spread

Trading

The gap between bid and ask. Narrow spreads usually mean a liquid stock.

स्प्रेड

Stock split

Basics

Dividing existing shares into more shares. The price per share falls proportionally; the total value stays the same.

स्टॉक स्प्लिट

Stop-loss order

Trading

An order that becomes a market or limit order when price reaches a set level, to limit a loss. It is not guaranteed to fill at that level.

स्टॉप-लॉस ऑर्डर

Strike price

Derivatives

The price at which an option can be exercised.

स्ट्राइक प्राइस

STT (Securities Transaction Tax)

Indian market

A tax charged by the government on securities transactions, varying by trade type.

STT (सिक्योरिटीज़ ट्रांज़ैक्शन टैक्स)

Support

Technical analysis

A price level where buying has tended to appear and stop further falls.

सपोर्ट

Swing trading

Trading

Holding trades for days to weeks to capture a price swing.

स्विंग ट्रेडिंग

Trading account

Indian market

The account used to place buy and sell orders through a broker.

ट्रेडिंग खाता

Trend

Technical analysis

The general direction of price: up (higher highs and lows), down (lower highs and lows), or sideways.

ट्रेंड

Volatility

Basics

How much and how quickly a price moves. Higher volatility means bigger swings in both directions.

अस्थिरता (वोलैटिलिटी)

Volume

Basics

The number of shares traded in a period. High volume means more participation.

वॉल्यूम

Working capital

Fundamentals

Current assets minus current liabilities. The money available to run daily operations.

वर्किंग कैपिटल
