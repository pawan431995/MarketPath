# MarketPath: market analysis and content plan

**Basis:** written from general knowledge of these books and sites (no live research). Verify prices, features and availability before relying on them. Last reviewed for this plan: 2026.

## 1. Books in the market

| Level | Book | Author | What it teaches | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Beginner | The Psychology of Money | Morgan Housel | Behaviour, patience, luck vs risk | Anyone starting, to build the right mindset |
| Beginner | Let's Talk Money | Monika Halan | Indian personal finance, insurance, SIP, goals | Indian readers starting out |
| Beginner | The Little Book of Common Sense Investing | John Bogle | Low-cost index investing | Long-term, passive investors |
| Beginner | A Random Walk Down Wall Street | Burton Malkiel | Market efficiency, index funds, asset allocation | Learning why most active trading underperforms |
| Intermediate | The Intelligent Investor | Benjamin Graham | Value investing, margin of safety | Serious long-term stock investors |
| Intermediate | One Up On Wall Street | Peter Lynch | Finding stocks in everyday life, categories of companies | Fundamental research beginners |
| Intermediate | The Little Book That Beats the Market | Joel Greenblatt | Magic formula (quality plus value) | Systematic stock selection |
| Intermediate | Common Stocks and Uncommon Profits | Philip Fisher | Qualitative analysis of growth companies | Growth-stock researchers |
| Intermediate | Japanese Candlestick Charting Techniques | Steve Nison | Candlestick patterns and their use | Chart readers |
| Intermediate | Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets | John Murphy | Trends, indicators, chart patterns | Technical analysis students |
| Advanced | Trading in the Zone | Mark Douglas | Trader psychology, discipline, probability thinking | Traders with recurring emotional mistakes |
| Advanced | Market Wizards | Jack Schwager | Interviews with top traders and their methods | Seeing different trading styles |
| Advanced | Reminiscences of a Stock Operator | Edwin Lefèvre | Market history and speculation (fictionalised) | Understanding market behaviour and mistakes |
| Advanced | Options as a Strategic Investment | Lawrence McMillan | Option strategies, Greeks, risk | Options traders |
| Advanced | Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives | John Hull | Derivatives theory and pricing | Academic and professional depth |

Pattern: books split into **mindset** (Housel, Halan, Douglas), **investing** (Graham, Lynch, Bogle), and **trading tools** (Murphy, Nison, McMillan, Hull). Most learners need the first two groups before the third.

Pattern: the big sites win on **depth (glossaries, courses), tools (charts, calculators, simulators), and data (live numbers, company ratios, news)**. Free, structured Indian content is well covered by Varsity and NSE Academy.

## 4. What this means

Learners stay on a site when it answers the next question: "what does this word mean", "what do I read next", "can I check this", "can I practise". A static site can do the first four well. It cannot offer live prices, news, or a brokerage-grade simulator without a backend or a data provider.

## 5. Plan, in priority order

1. **Glossary** (done in this round): expand to about 70 terms, EN and HI, searchable.
2. **Reading list** (removed from the site by request). The books and sites in sections 1 and 2 are used only as background when writing lesson content. Learners should not need to leave MarketPath.
3. **Quiz** (done): self-test across all topics with explanations, EN and HI.
4. **Calculators** (done): goal SIP planner, lumpsum growth, CAGR, break-even cost for trades.
5. **Indian market rules** (done): the Market Rules & Investor Protection module covers timings, settlement, circuit limits, KYC and nomination, IPOs, registered advice versus tips, scam signs and how to complain. Rules change, so check SEBI and exchange circulars before each update.
6. **Paper-trading simulator** (done): virtual account with simulated prices for every NSE stock, charts, indicators, drawing tools, Indian charges and tax. Live NSE data would need a licensed provider.
7. **Live data, news, community** (not planned): these need a backend or licensed data. Recommend linking to official sources (NSE, BSE, SEBI) for live information.

## 6. Content rules we follow

- Educational only. No buy or sell calls, no guaranteed returns.
- Examples use illustrative numbers and say so.
- Every page works in English and Hindi.
- Official sources (SEBI, NSE, AMFI) are the reference for rules and figures.
